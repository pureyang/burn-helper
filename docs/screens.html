Burn Helper screens

Every screen on the badge and the button that gets you there. Short presses act when you let go. B steps through the three modes in a loop; A and C do something different in each.

Animation

The figure speeds up with the number of agents working, through 15 levels up to hyperspace past 100. Hearts show the 5-hour usage left, and a speech bubble pops up when a session is waiting for you.

This is the only screen where A / C change the theme.

From any mode

Hold B 2 s

Phone setup: the badge becomes a hotspot. Scan the code, open the address, add a Wi-Fi network. B cancels. Back to the animation when done.

Hold UP 2 s

Demo mode on or off: the hero theme climbs through every level in 30 seconds, with a DEMO tag.

DOWN

Brightness, 10 levels. After the dimmest comes 0%: a 3-second note, then the screen goes off while the badge keeps running. Press again for full brightness.

Hold DOWN 2 s

Case lights on or off. They are off by default.

Hold UP + DOWN 3 s

The hidden 20-second take for the launch video: like the demo, with no tag.

UP on Debug page 3

Rescan and retry Wi-Fi right away.

HOME

The stock app menu. HOME again comes back.
